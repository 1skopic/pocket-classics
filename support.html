Support

Questions, problems or ideas: we're happy to help.

Get in touch

Open an issue at github.com/1skopic/pocket-classics/issues. Please mention your phone model and what happened.

Missing a game? Use Suggest a game in the app's settings.

Common questions

I bought the unlock but the games are locked again. Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. The unlock also works on your other devices with that account, and with Family Sharing on the App Store.

Will I lose my progress if I change phones? Progress and stats are kept on the device only, since there is no account. Your purchase moves with your Apple ID or Google account.

Does the app work offline? Yes, every game works without a connection.

Refunds. Purchases are handled by Apple and Google. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com or through Google Play's order history.

Pocket Classics is made by one independent developer.
