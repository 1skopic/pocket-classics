Pocket Classics

A calm collection of more than 50 classic puzzle, card, word and arcade games in one small app.

No ads, no tracking, no account and no subscription. Every game has a free daily puzzle, a few games are free forever, and one purchase unlocks everything, including games added later.

Available for iPhone and iPad on the App Store and for Android on Google Play.

Pocket Classics is made by one independent developer.
