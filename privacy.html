Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Pocket Classics is built to respect your privacy. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no account.

What stays on your device

Your progress, scores, statistics and settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them. If your phone backs up apps (iCloud backup on iPhone, Android backup to your Google account), they are part of that backup, which Apple or Google keep for you under their own privacy policies. Deleting the app deletes them from the device.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment; we never see your name, payment details or account. The app only checks with the store whether the unlock was bought.

Game suggestions

If you choose to use Suggest a game, the app sends the game name and details you typed, and whether you use iPhone or Android, to our server. Nothing else is attached: no name, account or device ID. Your internet address is used only to prevent spam, kept in memory as a one-way scrambled code for at most 24 hours, and never stored with your suggestion. Please don't type personal information in a suggestion. Suggestions are kept for as long as they help us decide which games to make. Because they aren't linked to you, we can't find or delete a single person's suggestion later.

Featured games

When the app starts, and at most once an hour while it is open, it downloads the current list of featured and new games from our server. That request contains nothing about you or how you play. Our server sees your internet address only to answer it and does not log or store it.

Where the data goes

Our server is rented from Hetzner Online, a German hosting company, and is only reached over an encrypted connection. Nothing is passed on to anyone else.

What we don't do

We don't collect personal data, don't use advertising or tracking, and don't share or sell anything to anyone.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/1skopic/pocket-classics/issues.

Pocket Classics is made by one independent developer.
